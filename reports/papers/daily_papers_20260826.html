Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness

WorldToken: Time-First Sequence Modeling for Robotic Imitation Learning

How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently

ConvergeFlow: Language Flow with Provable Convergence to Token Embeddings
连续扩散/流式语言模型已能达到与离散 LM 相当的性能，但现有连续框架仍缺乏收敛性保证。ConvergeFlow 提出一种可证明收敛到 token embedding 的语言流，为连续语言模型的训练提供了理论根基。
